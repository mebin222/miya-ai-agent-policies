Privacy Policy

Miya AI Agent

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Miya AI Agent ("the App") processes information when its operator connects it to supported Instagram and Meta services. The App provides tools for managing Instagram conversations and comments, preparing replies, and supporting account content workflows.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled and the permissions granted, the App may process:

  • Instagram account and profile information made available through authorized Meta APIs.
  • Conversation messages, sender or recipient identifiers, and related messaging metadata available through the API.
  • Comments, comment identifiers, post or media information, captions, and related engagement metadata.
  • Webhook event metadata sent by Meta when an enabled event occurs.
  • Text submitted to AI assistance features to prepare suggested replies or other content.

The App's access depends on the permissions granted by Meta. It does not intentionally request information beyond its configured features and authorized access.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Display and organize Instagram conversations and comments.
  • Prepare suggested replies and content using enabled AI tools.
  • Support authorized publishing and messaging actions initiated by the account operator.
  • Receive and diagnose webhook events and troubleshoot errors.
  • Protect the App and maintain its security.

4. AI Processing

AI-assisted features may process the text supplied to them to generate draft content or suggested replies. The intended local configuration uses Ollama on the operator's own computer. If the operator configures a third-party AI service or another hosted model, the submitted information may be processed by that provider under its applicable terms and privacy policy.

AI-generated content may be inaccurate and should be reviewed by the account operator before use.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

The App does not sell personal information. Information may be processed or disclosed as necessary to:

  • Use Meta and Instagram services requested by the account operator.
  • Use hosting, infrastructure, or AI providers configured for the deployment.
  • Comply with applicable laws or respond to valid legal requests.
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the App.

6. Storage and Retention

The amount and duration of stored information depend on the deployment and enabled features. The current receive-only webhook setup records limited event metadata in server logs and does not intentionally log message text or send automatic replies. The App is not intended to maintain a permanent central archive of Instagram messages by default.

Server logs, temporary processing data, and configuration information may remain for as long as reasonably necessary for operation, troubleshooting, and security.

7. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect account credentials, access tokens, webhook secrets, and processed information. Operators should keep secrets out of source code and public repositories, restrict access to configuration files, and promptly revoke compromised credentials.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Data Requests

The connected account operator can manage permissions through Meta, disconnect integrations, and revoke access tokens. Requests concerning information processed by the App can be made through the operator's official Miya Arabelle Instagram account. Requests may require verification so information is not disclosed to the wrong person.

Disconnecting the App does not necessarily delete information already retained in logs or by Meta and other providers. Such information must be addressed with the relevant operator or provider where applicable.

9. Third-Party Services

Meta, Instagram, hosting platforms, and any configured AI providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. This policy does not replace those documents or govern their independent processing of information.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is an account-management utility and is not designed specifically for children. Its operator should not use it to knowingly collect personal information from children unlawfully.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, integrations, or data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning the App, contact its operator through the official Miya Arabelle Instagram account. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other security secrets in a privacy request.
